5 · Question Bank — what it is, what it costs L3 · your call

Plain words: a shared library of reusable questions. You write "How satisfied are you with support?" once, push it to the bank, and every future survey can insert it from the bank. Because every survey then asks the identical question, you can chart "support satisfaction across all surveys over time" — answers become comparable by construction instead of by luck.
The lift: ≈ 2–3 weeks, after v1 survey slices ship. One new object (the bank question: label, type, config, topic), a "Push to bank" button in the properties column (§1b), a "From Question Bank" palette source (shown dimmed in §1), and a link from each answer back to its bank question — that link is what powers cross-survey charts. The one real design decision: editing a bank question freezes published usages (they keep the old wording) — silently rewording a live question would corrupt trend lines.

7 · Will surveys look like our forms? (your check — verified)

You spotted that the survey demos look nothing like the current form render. Correct — and traced, not assumed:

Why they differ: every survey demo (this page, survey_question_types_ux.html, survey_renderings_ux.html) is hand-styled illustration HTML — zero of the product's --c-* theme tokens appear in them (grep-verified), while the real form renderer consumes those tokens everywhere. Demos show ideas; they don't wear the product's paint.
The guarantee in the plan: catalog principle 2.0.3 — every survey widget consumes the same theme tokens as today's form fields (accent, field surfaces, radius scale, focus ring), no private colors allowed. Surveys render inside the same viewer, sections, layouts, and 30-theme catalog. In the "plain widgets" pack, a survey looks exactly like your forms do today.
And the builder is the same builder: §1 above now mirrors the live build page — blueprint chips in the middle, truth on the right, properties via the palette-column swap. Rev 1 of this mockup drew a generic WYSIWYG canvas with a permanent right inspector; the owner caught it (2026-07-27). Survey authoring adds a Questions palette and new chip types — it does NOT invent a second builder.
And that's Q9 again: if same-as-forms is what you want by default, rule "plain widgets default" — Card Deck (deliberately different, one-big-card-per-question) stays one click away. If you want surveys to feel like a different product, make Card Deck the default. Your call closes §10.
