A · Edit in the panel

Every fill is a small block right in the inspector: which Case field, where its value comes from, and the value.
  • Everything visible at once; no extra click.
  • Gets long with 4–5 fills (each block is about 150px tall).
  • The value box follows the field's type: a picklist offers its values, a date a date box, a lookup a record search.
