Related lists on a record page: pick a look

Same Contact, same Cases, three ways to draw them. "Open cases" can be edited and deleted from. "Closed cases" is read only.

A · Cards. What groups look like today, one card per Case. Roomy and works with any number of fields. Gets long fast: 10 Cases is a lot of scrolling.

B · Table. Looks like a Salesforce related list: one line per Case. Most compact and easiest to scan. Comfortable up to about 5 fields; more than that gets cramped. Needs the real Table built (today it says "coming soon").

C · Rows that open. One line per Case; click a line to open its fields. Compact and still fits any number of fields. Costs one extra click to edit, and you only see one Case's fields at a time.
