What changed, and why

Same data, same choices and same steps as today. The changes are words, pictures, color and order.

  1. A
    One sentence under the titleSays what Mapping is for before you read anything else.
  2. B
    "How it works" stripThree pictures: submit, steps run in order, answers fill fields. Can be hidden, and stays hidden.
  3. C
    Steps read as a sequenceNumbered dots on a line, because order really matters here. "Not finished" becomes "2 things to fix" or "Ready".
  4. D
    "In plain words" summaryEach step says what it will do in one sentence, and it updates as you change the choices.
  5. E
    Picture tiles for "Always create / Find or create"Replaces the button pair at the top right. Changes a control type, so it needs your OK.
  6. F
    Questions as headings"How do we spot an existing Account?" plus a tip on what to match on. Column labels in sentence case.
  7. G
    "If we find one" as picture tilesSame two choices, with a lock and a pencil. The public-form caution shrinks to a strip that names the safe choice.
  8. H
    Field ← answer arrows, missing required in redSays why Account Name matters and suggests an answer that fits.
  9. I
    "Where each answer goes" with colored tagsEach record gets a color, so you see at a glance which answers feed which record. Unmapped answers say "Kept on the submission only".
  10. J
    Footer says what's left"Step 1 has 2 things to fix" next to Done.

Record colors bend design rule 6 ("Studio chrome stays neutral; teal only for action, selection, focus"). Flip the switch at the top to compare with neutral.
